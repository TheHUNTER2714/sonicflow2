1 Install a Cookie Exporter Extension

Install one of the following open-source browser extensions:

Chrome / Brave / Edge: Get cookies.txt LOCALLY ↗ Firefox: Cookie-Editor ↗
2 Export YouTube Cookies

1. Open youtube.com in your browser (Incognito or standard account) and verify a video plays normally.
2. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Click "Export" or "Export As Netscape" to download cookies.txt.

3 Activate Immediately (No Redeploy Needed)

Switch to the Upload File tab above and drop your cookies.txt. It takes effect in 1 second without requiring a Render restart!

4 Permanent Cloud Persistence (Optional: Render Secret Files)

To make your cookies permanent even across fresh Render git redeployments:
1. Go to your Render Dashboard ➔ Services ➔ sonicflow2 ➔ Environment.
2. Scroll down to Secret Files and click "Add Secret File".
3. Filename: cookies.txt.
4. File Contents: Paste the contents of your cookies.txt.
SonicFlow automatically checks /etc/secrets/cookies.txt on startup.
